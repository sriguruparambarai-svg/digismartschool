Teacher's Week ← School Admin
One page per class for each school day. Teachers put a ✗ on the roll number of every child who did not do the homework, did not write the class test, was absent for it, or came without nails cut, notes maintained, shoes, uniform or haircut — and tick whether they corrected the homework. The office collects the sheets, photographs them and uploads them in ERP → Homework Defaulters → 📷 Sheet photo. Parents get the messages at 4 PM with the homework.
