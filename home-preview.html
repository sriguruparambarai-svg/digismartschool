Monitors · My School’s Academics

See every classroom without walking the corridors

Your ERP knows fees and attendance. It does not know whether Chapter 4 was taught today. This does.

Period 3, Saturday: each class, its teacher, the exact pages being taught — and who is free if someone is absent.

1

The portion is planned to the last day of term

Pick a class and subject. The AI reads your own textbook — NCERT, Samacheer or your school’s book — and spreads it over every period up to the term end, skipping holidays.

  • No more unfinished portion — you see from day one when each chapter will be done, and which class is falling behind.
  • Sudden holiday? One click and every plan moves.
2

Every teacher is told exactly what to teach — and reports back

On her phone, or on a printed sheet: each period’s pages, the steps to teach, and the homework to give. By 3:30 PM she reports what was done.

  • “Done” means done — a quick class quiz on the portion shows whether the children really learnt it.
  • Notebook checks by you or the office, class by class.
3

Homework comes from the lesson taught — and is checked

An ERP only sends what a teacher types. Here the homework comes from that day’s lesson, never too much in one day, and reaches parents at 4 PM with what was taught in class.

  • You see whether it was given and corrected — before parents complain.
  • Teachers’ own homework waits for your OK.
Teaches · TeachBot

A teacher is absent? The class still learns.

TeachBot is an AI teacher that takes the class — not a video, not a quiz app. It teaches the same chapter the class is on, one step at a time, on the board.

  • Speaks Tamil and English. Book words stay in English, exactly as printed.
  • Maths worked on the board — every step shown, with the figure drawn.
  • 3D science and geography models students can turn and zoom.
  • Weak students get the lesson again, as many times as they need.
Manages · DigiSmart ERP

The office runs itself, and parents stay informed

Fees, attendance, admissions, salary, exams and transport in one place — with parents told on WhatsApp, in English and Tamil.

  • Fee dues reminders, receipts, bills and Tally export.
  • Absent today? Parents are told the same day.
  • TN State Board report cards and I-cards, names in Tamil and English.
