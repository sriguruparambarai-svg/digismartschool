How it works

Plan once. The school follows it every day.

No more chasing teachers for lesson plans, diaries or homework. DigiSmart turns your textbooks and timetable into each day’s work — for the teacher, the principal and the parents.

1

Plan the whole term in minutes

Pick a class and subject. The AI reads your textbook — NCERT, Samacheer or your own book — and plans every period up to the term end.

  • Chapter by chapter, page by page — teaching, class work, homework and class test.
  • Holidays skipped automatically. A sudden holiday? One click moves every plan.
  • Maths from worked Examples, with the matching exercise sums as homework.
2

Every teacher knows her day

On her phone — or on a printed sheet if she prefers paper. For every period: the class, the pages, the steps to teach and the exact homework to give.

  • Teacher app with her own link and PIN — she ticks lessons as taught.
  • Teacher’s Week printout — one page a day, with a Done box and signature line.
  • Each teacher sees only what you allow.
3

Homework reaches parents at 4 PM

The week’s homework and class work fill themselves from the lesson plans, following your homework timetable. You glance at it once — parents get it in the student diary at 4 PM.

  • Never too much in a day — the planner warns you.
  • Teachers’ own homework waits for your OK before it goes.
  • Parents see class work too — what was taught today.

Included · AI teacher

TeachBot — an AI teacher that takes the class

Not a video and not a quiz app. TeachBot stands at the board and teaches the chapter the class is on, one step at a time, in the language your class understands.

  • Speaks Tamil and English. Book words stay in English, exactly as printed.
  • Maths worked on the board. Every step shown, with the figure drawn.
  • 3D science and geography. Models students can turn and zoom.
  • Question papers and worksheets from your own textbook.
