🌍 Real World Examples — Tamil Nadu
🏗️ Construction — House Corner Check
A construction worker in Mayiladuthurai wants to check if a room corner is exactly 90°. He measures 3 metres along one wall and 4 metres along the other. If the diagonal is exactly 5 metres — the corner is perfect!
🚢 Ship Navigation — Chennai Port
A ship leaves Chennai port going 6 km East, then turns and goes 8 km North. How far is it from the starting point? Using Pythagorean theorem: √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 km!
📺 Television Screen Size
When we say a TV is "32 inch" — that is the diagonal measurement! If the screen is 25 inches wide and 19 inches tall, the diagonal = √(25² + 19²) = √(625 + 361) = √986 ≈ 31.4 inches
