Step 1: Select Class, Subject, Chapter — paste the lesson text from the textbook.

Chat Mode: Students ask doubts — Malar Teacher answers ONLY from the lesson, not from outside.

Quiz Mode: 10 MCQ questions auto-generated from the lesson to test students.
