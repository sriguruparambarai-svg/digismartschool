ℹ️ How to Use
  1. Select Class, Subject and type the Chapter name.
  2. Upload a PDF or paste the lesson text in the box above.
  3. Optionally upload images related to the lesson.
  4. Click Activate — students can now use the LearnBot for this lesson.
  5. Teaching Mode: Avatar teacher explains the lesson slide by slide with voice.
  6. Chat Mode: Students can ask any question about the lesson.
  7. Quiz Mode: 10 MCQ questions are auto-generated from the lesson.
