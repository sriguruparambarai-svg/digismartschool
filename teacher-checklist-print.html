Teacher's Weekly Checklist

📷 Teachers' Report ← School Admin
One sheet per teacher for the week. Each evening she writes ✓ (done) or ✗ (not done) for every class: portion done as planned, yesterday's homework corrected, revision done, class test held — and any complaints. Boxes with — don't apply that day. She hands the sheet to the office on Saturday; the office photographs it in 📷 Teachers' Report. Scores show in My School's Academics → Teachers, and a monthly evaluation goes to the owner. Teachers who use the Teacher App don't need this sheet — their “Submit my day” is used instead.
