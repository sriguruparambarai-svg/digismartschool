DigiSmart Answer Cards

Each child gets one card with their own number. To answer, they turn the card so their letter is at the top and hold it up. The facilitator's phone reads the whole class at once.

  1. Print on thick paper (card sheet) if you can. Do not laminate: shiny cards reflect light and are hard to read.
  2. Cut along the dashed lines. Give each child the card with their roll number, and keep the same card for the whole year.
  3. Teach the children: find your letter, turn the card until that letter is at the top, hold it up with the black pattern facing the teacher, and keep your fingers off the black pattern.
